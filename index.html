Our story

From the farm at Pawna to your table in Baner.

Samspire started with a simple idea: mushrooms deserve to be the star of the plate, not a side. We grow our own near Pawna Dam, where the cool, damp hills are just right for them, and bring them to Baner fresh.

Every dish on our menu is built around mushrooms, from crispy oyster "wings" to a slow-stirred wild mushroom risotto and a lion's mane latte. It's comfort food that happens to be good for you.
